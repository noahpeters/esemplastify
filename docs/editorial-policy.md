# Editorial scope

Esemplastify shapes diverse information into a connected daily listening experience. AI is a production tool, not the subject of the show. The working emphasis is curiosity: creation, living, learning, and world context.

## Selection

- Discover across world affairs, science, nature, design and making, culture, history, ideas, economics, health, everyday life, and technology. No AI keyword is required for admission.
- Prefer a range of subjects and independent publishers. The explainable ranker gives unseen subjects a stronger preference until five available subjects are represented. This is a preference, not a guarantee when quotas or source availability constrain the pool.
- Allow at most two stories per subject and two per publisher. An evidence-linked story counts against every publisher contributing to it. Allow at most one AI story, with no requirement to include any and no AI relevance bonus.
- Broad feeds need title-level classification: a story about furniture from a science or technology publisher can belong to design. Labels are heuristics for editorial review, not semantic understanding or factual verification.
- Preserve initial freshness, source provenance, clustering, and the single substantive, reviewed 24–48-hour verification follow-up. Do not fill runtime with repetitions or weak material.

The catalog includes BBC, Al Jazeera, Quanta, NASA, Nature, MIT, Phys.org, Smithsonian, Dezeen, Designboom, Colossal, NPR, The Guardian, Aeon, The Marginalian, Ars Technica, Rest of World, The Conversation, and NIST. Shared publishers count together. The initial mix is English-language and does not claim comprehensive geographic or viewpoint representation.

## Production and history

The automated ranker proposes a lineup. An editor selects and checks the stories and writes the script, with Codex assistance in the pilot workflow. Workers AI currently narrates reviewed scripts; it does not autonomously choose stories or write scripts.

The first private pilot used the wrong AI-only scope. It remains a historical private draft, not an approved model for future episodes. Old episode snapshots and evidence are immutable. Retired narrow AI feeds are disabled for new ingestion; their evidence stays available, including for legitimate historical follow-ups. Never infer actual coverage from a draft or a rendered MP3.
